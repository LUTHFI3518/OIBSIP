1955 – 2011

Steve Jobs

The pioneer of the personal computer revolution and digital era.

"Design is not just what it looks like and feels like. Design is how it works."

His Story

Steve Jobs was an American inventor, designer, and entrepreneur who co-founded Apple Computer and served as its CEO. Through his vision, focus, and pursuit of simplicity, he revolutionized multiple industries, including personal computing, animated movies, music, phones, tablet computing, and digital publishing.

His insistence on design elegance and user-centric engineering transformed the way humanity interacts with technology, turning digital devices into extensions of personal creativity.

Milestones & Legacy

  • 1955
    Born in San Francisco, California. Adopted by Paul and Clara Jobs.
  • 1976
    Co-founded Apple Computer with Steve Wozniak in his family garage, launching the Apple I.
  • 1984
    Introduced the original Macintosh, popularizing the graphical user interface for mass markets.
  • 1985
    Resigned from Apple following a board struggle, then founded NeXT Computer and bought the computer graphics group that became Pixar.
  • 1997
    Returned to Apple as CEO after the acquisition of NeXT, leading Apple back from the brink of bankruptcy.
  • 2001
    Unveiled the iPod and the iTunes Store, transforming the music industry into a digital ecosystem.
  • 2007
    Introduced the iPhone, redefining mobile communication, internet browsing, and touch screens.
  • 2010
    Launched the iPad, establishing the consumer tablet computer market.
  • 2011
    Passed away on October 5, leaving behind a legacy that shaped the modern world.
